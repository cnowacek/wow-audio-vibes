Music for every corner of Azeroth: every city, zone, dungeon, raid and PvP map gets its own music, with a Relaxed and a Locked-in playlist for each.

## Install
1. Download **WoWAudioVibes-win-Setup.exe** below and run it. It installs for your Windows user, no admin needed. If Windows SmartScreen warns about an unrecognised app, choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
2. Double-click the companion's tray icon, open the **WoW addon** tab and click **Install** next to your WoW.
3. Restart WoW completely (exit to desktop). Type `/vibes` in game.

## What's in it
- Works without Spotify: free internet radio stations picked for each zone's vibe, or folders of your own music.
- Spotify is optional and needs Premium plus your own free Spotify developer app; the Spotify tab in Settings walks you through it.
- A Settings window for everything; no config files to edit.
- The companion installs and updates the addon for you.
- The companion updates itself.
