More fitting radio for every zone, and an addon that knows what's playing.

## What's new
- **25 radio stations instead of 11, picked per vibe.** Native American music in the Barrens, Durotar and Mulgore, Middle Eastern music in the deserts, African folk in Stranglethorn and Un'Goro, dungeon synth in dungeons, medieval music in the Alliance cities and folk metal in Orgrimmar. Undercity is now spooky. If a station can't be reached, a similar one plays instead.
- **Next, Previous and Like go grey on radio.** Radio is live, so there's no track to skip to; the button's tooltip says why. The player bar and the addon's Settings show where the music comes from (internet radio, a music folder or Spotify).

## Updating
The companion updates itself. Restart WoW completely afterwards so the updated addon loads.

New here? Download **WoWAudioVibes-win-Setup.exe** below, run it, double-click the tray icon, open the **WoW addon** tab and click **Install**.
