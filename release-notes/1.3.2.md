Fixes a loud buzz when pausing radio.

## Fixed
- **Pausing radio or folder music buzzed.** Instead of silence, the last moment of music repeated over and over. The same bug could buzz briefly when a radio stream stalled or between tracks in a music folder. Pauses are silent now.

## Updating
The companion updates itself; this update doesn't change the addon.

New here? Download **WoWAudioVibes-win-Setup.exe** below, run it, double-click the tray icon, open the **WoW addon** tab and click **Install**.
